# The Gate of Ararich

*Two Chosen Teens. One Dangerous Secret Across Worlds.*

**The Gate of Ararich** is a young adult fantasy about parallel worlds and magical adventures.

Olivia never wanted to be chosen. Andre never believed in destiny. Yet when a strange artifact finds them, their lives split apart, pulling them beyond Earth into parallel realms, forgotten civilizations, and distant planets where the laws of existence twist into something unrecognizable.

Hunted by the relentless Consortium and guided by forces that speak in riddles, they journey through starborn empires, forgotten races, and encounters with angels who have witnessed the rise and ruin of countless worlds.

*The question is no longer what the Gate of Ararich is. It is what the universe will become when the Gate finally opens.*

## About the Author

Irena has always been drawn to fairy tales, fantasy, and the mysterious worlds that exist beyond the boundaries of ordinary imagination. A lawyer by profession, she spends her days in the realities of the material world, while writing allows her to step through an unseen doorway into worlds where magic, adventure, and the impossible come alive.

Perhaps every extraordinary journey begins with nothing more than the courage to face the unknown.
