The Gate of Ararich

Two Chosen Teens. One Dangerous Secret Across Worlds.

What if the world you trust is only one fragment of a vast, hidden lattice of realities… and its collapse has already begun?

Olivia never wanted to be chosen. Andre never believed in destiny.

Yet when a strange artifact finds them, their lives split apart, pulling them beyond Earth into parallel realms, forgotten civilizations, and distant planets where the laws of existence twist into something unrecognizable.

Hunted by the relentless Consortium and guided by forces that speak in riddles, Olivia and Andre move through shifting dimensions where worlds flicker out, timelines fracture, and entire realities struggle to stay alive. To save what remains, they must journey through starborn empires, forgotten races, and speak with angels who have witnessed the rise and ruin of countless worlds. With every encounter, the fabric of reality thins, revealing a truth that was never meant to surface.

The question is no longer what the Gate of Ararich is. It is what the universe will become when the Gate finally opens.
