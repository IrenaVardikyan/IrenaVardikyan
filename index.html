Hi, I'm Irena.

Author of The Gate of Ararich. YA fantasy, parallel worlds and magical adventures.

I've always been drawn to fairy tales, fantasy, and the mysterious worlds that exist beyond the boundaries of ordinary imagination. I'm a lawyer by profession, so my days are spent in the realities of the material world. Writing lets me step through an unseen doorway into worlds where magic, adventure, and the impossible come alive.

With a lifelong fascination for other realms and extraordinary characters, I invite you to follow two teenagers on a journey beyond everything they have ever known. Perhaps every extraordinary journey begins with nothing more than the courage to face the unknown.
